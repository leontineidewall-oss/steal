07 faq
what do I get for holding the tag?

the coin's creator fees, for as long as you hold it. they go out from the tag wallet with memo fee.

why does the ask melt?

so nobody can park it at a price no one pays. hold it long and it gets cheap to take.

what if two people steal at once?

the first one on chain wins. the second paid under the new ask, so it's void and sent back.

can I steal it back?

yes, any time, at the ask. you can't steal from yourself.
